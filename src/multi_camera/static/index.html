Comprehensive operational guide for multi-camera forensic tracking

🎯 Core Surveillance Tracking Workflow

  1. Acquire Feeds: Use Live for webcams or click Load Videos to auto-load camera folders.
  2. Lock Focus: Left-Click on any detected person bounding box to lock target and seed appearance gallery.
  3. Capture Viewpoints: When target turns or changes lighting, press A to add angle crops. (2–4 diverse angles ensure superior cross-camera matching).
  4. Autonomous Recovery: When the target leaves the camera, Argus automatically expands search radius (R=1 → R=2) across adjacent cameras in the network graph.
  5. Target Correction: If tracking latches onto a bystander in dense crowds, simply Left-Click the actual target to correct it, or press Ctrl+Z to undo.
